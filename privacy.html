Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 June 2026

This privacy policy applies to the internal application “Esnad Live Pull” (“the App”), operated by Syed Zaigham Ali on behalf of Esnad Africa Oil Services and Operations LLC-FZ (“we”, “us”). The App is used solely to retrieve the company’s own accounting data from QuickBooks Online for internal reporting.

1. Information We Access

The App connects to QuickBooks Online via the official Intuit API using OAuth 2.0. With your authorization, it reads accounting data such as reports, invoices, customers, and account balances belonging to the connected QuickBooks company. The App does not collect data from any third parties.

2. How We Use Information

Accessed data is used only to display and analyze the company’s financial information internally (for example, in spreadsheets and dashboards). We do not sell, rent, or share this data with any third party.

3. Data Storage and Security

Authentication tokens are stored securely in the App’s server-side configuration and are never exposed publicly. Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the App from within QuickBooks Online.

4. Data Retention

Retrieved data is held only as long as needed for internal reporting and may be deleted at any time. Disconnecting the App invalidates all access tokens immediately.
